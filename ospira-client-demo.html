Part 3 — A Difference of Perceptions

Where self-view and parent view diverge most

Every score is captured twice — once from the child, once from each parent. The gap itself becomes the coaching material, not a "wrong" answer on either side.

Child's self-score Parent's score
Why the gap happens

A child may show a strength quietly, privately, or only outside the home — the parent simply hasn't seen it yet. Every gap ships with a "why," an impact note for both sides, and a short action checklist.

If gaps go unaddressed
  • Short-term: communication hiccups, feeling unappreciated.
  • Medium-term: the child shares less, study-habit friction.
  • Long-term: stifled talents, strained trust.
